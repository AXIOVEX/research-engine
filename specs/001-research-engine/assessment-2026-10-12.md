## F-001-D6 — disposition in full

**The inoperability is discharged; the comparative test remains unrun; the falsifier remains live.**

What the executed K3 protocol establishes:

1. **A K3 protocol now exists.** Pre-registered thresholds were recorded by amendment *before* the evaluation freeze (K3 budget: 2 hours per question, G3/OD-5), the audit procedure is specified (fresh-context reviewer, bundle alone, named retrace elements per claim), and the protocol was executed end-to-end exactly once, on the Phase 7 bundle (§2.3). NC-5's condition — "inoperable until a K3 protocol exists" — is met. F-001-D6 can now fire; before 2026-10-12 it could not.
2. **The per-claim register arm performed under that protocol.** A reviewer with no prior exposure retraced all five claims — statement + falsifier, verdict + interval + reason, evidence items + origin groups + sufficiency floor, citation outcomes, exclusions — from the bundle alone, verifier ok, in ~18 minutes against a 10-hour budget (~3%). Working at per-claim granularity, the auditor also surfaced defects a blended score structurally cannot express: a falsifier-flag contradicting its registration (CLM-E-004), and a wrong author given name in a registered citation (CIT-E-401). That is the information-preservation the claim asserts, observed in use.

What it does **not** establish — the transfer limits, stated plainly:

1. **No blended-score arm was ever fielded.** F-001-D6 fires if a blended score and the register lead an adjudicator to the *same decision quality and speed*. The executed audit measured the register arm only. Decision quality was never compared across arms; neither arm's decisions were scored against a ground truth. The comparative measurement the falsifier specifies has never been run, by this evaluation or any other.
2. **The execution was on Spec 002's bundle, not a Spec 001 study.** Spec 001 has no study of its own; its claims are product-level, and the evidence transfers as *the mechanism exercised under its own named protocol* — the same transfer basis on which CLM-001-01…04 earned `checked` at v0.3.0. It does not transfer as a Spec 001 audit result.
3. **The register's own fidelity defects are part of the record.** The same audit found the bundle's `claims.json` still carrying `status: "proposed"` for every claim and the CLM-E-004 falsifier flag false against its registration's language (§2.3 friction 2, 6). The register preserved the information; parts of its bookkeeping were wrong in ways only the audit caught. (The machinery items were taken up by Spec 011; the frozen Phase 7 bundle itself is, correctly, unaltered.)

Accordingly CLM-001-06 moves **created → checked** — the stage this program gives a claim whose mechanism has been built and exercised under its named test protocol, with the decisive comparative test still outstanding — and no further. `explained`, `accepted`, and `digested` are not approached. The named path to settling the claim is a two-arm K3 study: the same bundle audited under a blended-score presentation versus the per-claim register, decision quality and speed measured on both. Until someone runs it, F-001-D6 stands operable and unfired.

## What remains not understood

- **Whether the register beats a blended score** — the claim at the center of this re-assessment — is still unmeasured in its decisive respect. One single-arm audit, however strong, is corroboration, not the comparison.
- **Scale.** Every executed criterion ran on one frozen corpus (5 questions, 10 citations, 1 operator playing 4 actors — the single-operator caveat of the evaluation report applies to everything cited here). K1's zero-fabrication result, K4's folds, and K3's 18 minutes are measurements of that corpus, not of the product at institutional volume.
- **Drift efficacy (CLM-001-01)** remains the bundle's oldest unexercised limit: no live study has yet produced a post-freeze pressure event for the freeze mechanism to refuse.
- **Subtler fabrications.** K1 caught a nonexistent DOI and a corrupted citation; a real DOI attached to a wrong proposition is caught only as well as the lexical stance checker performs (evaluation report §2.1, §4.2) — unmeasured against adversarial cases.
- **Nothing in this bundle is explained or digested.** Lane 2 across Spec 001 still holds exactly one act: the owner's OD-1 acceptance of CLM-001-07. Every other claim in this bundle is machine-checked at best, and this re-assessment — itself an author's re-assessment of the author's own spec — does not change that.
