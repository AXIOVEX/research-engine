About this page

States the demo study's record does not contain (a dead claim, a pending adjudication, verdict badges on claims) are specified here as specimens — clearly not study data.

Component specimens

Design specimens — component states, not study data. Everything on the other wireframe screens is the Phase 5 demo study's real record; the states below exist in the machinery but not in that study, so they are specified here rather than fabricated there.

Verdict badges (claim-level, from a valid run)

SUPPORTED falsifier test ran, did not fire; interval clears the registered margin.

INCONCLUSIVE untested, below the sufficiency floor, interval straddles the margin — or the metadata-only cap (RT-5) binds.

CONTRADICTED the falsifier test fired, or the interval clears the margin in the contradicting direction.

Dead claim row (register treatment)

ClaimStatementStatusScore
CLM-X-000 DEAD Specimen statement — a withdrawn claim remains readable in the register. dead · reason: “withdrawn by the researcher: the question was superseded by a corrected registration” 0.0

Rule: a dead claim is never omitted and never shown as live. It renders muted, with the DEAD chip, the recorded reason verbatim, and score 0.0 (DN-005 dead semantics).

Pending adjudication item (decision form)

ADQ-000 quarantine PENDING

Specimen. Reason queued: metadata disagreement: year. Stage outcomes: resolution resolved · agreement disagree (year) · content metadata-only. Shown only on pending items, only in the adjudicator view.

A decision is an appended event. Deciding twice is refused by the engine: a changed decision is a new adjudication, not an edit.
