Admin console

Sign in to the admin console

The console has two planes, and they are disjoint: a platform sign-in holds no tenant powers, and a tenant sign-in reaches no other tenant (Spec 005 §5, CLM-005-09). Choose the plane you mean to work in — you will authenticate at that plane's identity provider.

Platform plane · deployment scope

Platform administration

Tenant lifecycle, platform configuration, break-glass policy, the deployment audit ledger. For the deployment's operators.

Continue to the deployment identity provider

Sign-in completes at the deployment IdP. There is no platform-plane password. Admin actions additionally require step-up: MFA evidence at the IdP, fresh within 10 minutes (recorded policy default).

Tenant plane · one tenant only

Tenant administration

Your institution's IdP configuration, membership, role grants, keys, and audit record. A session here can see no other tenant's records.

The tenant comes from your authenticated session, never from this picker alone — the picker only chooses which IdP you are sent to.
Continue to University A's identity provider

Your tenant's IdP (OIDC or SAML 2.0) is the only credential. There is no Tekmerion password — a screen asking for one is not this product (CLM-005-01).

How a refusal looks

Sign-in refused. auth.login_failed — SAML assertion audience mismatch (tenant uni-a, IdP https://idp-a.test/shibboleth). No session was created.
Refusals are stated as the service states them, and the refusal itself is appended to the tenant's audit ledger before it is shown (E2 discipline, T514). Specimen message — the shape is the requirement.
